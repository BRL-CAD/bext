glad 0.1.29 generated-code license evidence
===========================================

The generated files identify themselves as output from glad 0.1.29.  The
upstream README for that exact release states in its FAQ:

    What's the license of glad generated code? [#101]

    Any of Public Domain, WTFPL or CC0.

Source: https://github.com/Dav1dde/glad/blob/v0.1.29/README.md

For this SPDX declaration, bext selects the offered CC0-1.0 option for the
generated glad.c and glad.h files.

The separately bundled KHR/khrplatform.h retains a permission grant with the
same substantive terms accepted by the SPDX MIT template, including its
"Materials" alternatives.  The appleseed CMake integration also carries an
explicit MIT grant.  Those source notices are separately hash-pinned by the
bext SBOM metadata.
